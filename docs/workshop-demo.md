# One class cancelled. A clear next step for every guest.

[**Watch the 88-second film →**](https://blucca.github.io/raincheck/public/demo.html) · [YouTube](https://youtu.be/YuRDifk8XN8) · [Try the interactive preview](https://blucca.github.io/raincheck/)

The film follows a fictional pottery studio after a kiln repair forces it to cancel a Saturday class. Mina's two-person booking splits into one Sunday place for her sister and a full $68 ticket refund for Mina.

## What the film shows

1. **Read the guest's reply.** Raincheck proposes the split and checks Sunday's final open place against the saved seat ledger.
2. **Review and execute.** The operator selects the demo's interrupted-response scenario and runs the plan.
3. **Return after an interruption.** A page reload recovers the saved case. The Sunday seat stays held while the existing simulated refund is reconciled.
4. **Finish the handoff.** The settled outcome includes the replacement class, refund reference, customer reply, and downloadable event record.
5. **Keep the whole class in view.** Jules rebooks two tickets together. Priya receives a question with the remaining available times. The outcome ledger shows settled bookings, an open question, and Noah's booking awaiting review.

## Recording and modes

- Actual browser interactions were recorded on October 12, 2026. The edit combines those interactions with captured frames and explanatory graphics.
- All guests, bookings, original captures, and refunds in this film are fictional. Payments use Raincheck's simulated ledger; the interrupted response is an explicitly selected demonstration scenario.
- The preview replays [saved outputs from real AI calls](model-observations.md). New free-form replies use the live interpreter in a configured Node workspace.
- The separate [PayPal sandbox checkout](sandbox-guide.md) supports test-order creation, buyer approval, capture, and refund reconciliation. Its developer-account run is the next payment milestone.
- English narration is synthesized. Open captions are part of the film; an optional WebVTT track and a text transcript are available on the viewing page.
